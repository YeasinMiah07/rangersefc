Club Regulations

Rules & Guidelines

Every Ranger competes under one code — on the pitch and off it. These rules apply to all intra-club tournaments, club matches, and community league representation.

Code of Conduct Tournament Formats Match Rules Fair Play & Discipline Roster & Eligibility

🤝 Code of Conduct

  • Respect opponents, teammates, and admins at all times — in-game and in chat/voice.
  • No toxic behaviour, harassment, or discriminatory language. Zero tolerance.
  • Represent the Rangers badge with honour, win or lose.
  • Admin decisions on disputes are final during live tournaments.

⚽ Match Rules

  • Standard match length: 8-minute halves unless otherwise stated for the event.
  • Extra time + penalties apply to knockout stage draws; group/league stage draws stand.
  • Players must screenshot or record final scoreline and submit to admin within 30 minutes of match completion.
  • Disconnections: if a reconnect isn't possible within 5 minutes, the match is replayed from a 0-0 restart unless one side had a clear lead (admin discretion).
  • Custom formation/tactics restrictions may apply per tournament — check the specific event brief.

🛡️ Fair Play & Discipline

  • No use of exploits, lag-switching, or unofficial modifications. Instant disqualification.
  • No smurfing or playing under another member's account in official matches.
  • Repeated no-shows for scheduled matches may result in suspension from active tournaments.
  • Disputes must be raised with evidence (screenshots/clips) within 24 hours of the match.

👥 Roster & Eligibility

  • Main squad capacity: 24–30 active players out of a 40+ member club.
  • New signings undergo a trial period before being eligible for official community league matches (EFCOB/COBEG).
  • Players representing Rangers in external communities must maintain the club's code of conduct — you represent all of us.
  • Inactive players (no participation for 30+ days without notice) may be moved out of the main squad list.
