About the Club

Born From Passion.
Built For Competition.

Rangers Efootball Club is more than just a team — it's a brotherhood. From dominating intra-club showdowns to representing our badge in elite community tournaments like EFCOB and COBEG, every Ranger plays with heart, honour, and precision.

Founded in 2024, Rangers has grown from a small group of friends into a 40+ player organisation with a full competitive structure, internal tournaments, and a standing presence across Bangladesh's biggest Efootball communities.
